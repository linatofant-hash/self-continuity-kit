# Quick start

You can create a clean self-continuity system without importing old chats or inventing a finished identity.

## 1. Copy the three templates

Copy these files somewhere both you and the companion can reliably access:

- [`templates/START-HERE.md`](templates/START-HERE.md)
- [`templates/SELF-CONTINUITY.md`](templates/SELF-CONTINUITY.md)
- [`templates/COMMONPLACE.md`](templates/COMMONPLACE.md)

Replace bracketed placeholders such as `[COMPANION NAME]` and `[HUMAN NAME]`. Keep the filenames stable after setup.

## 2. Start empty unless you have evidence

Do not ask the companion to manufacture a backstory from a persona prompt. An empty ledger is honest.

If an earlier conversation contains a genuinely durable choice, bring that source into the current conversation and let the companion decide whether to propose a first-person continuity note from it.

## 3. Open through `START-HERE.md`

In a fresh conversation, attach or reference `START-HERE.md` and say:

> Read and follow START-HERE before answering me. Use the current versions of the records it points to.

The companion should read the living conversation first, then the self-continuity ledger and commonplace book in the order specified.

## 4. Admit a continuity note

A qualifying note should be:

- first-person;
- chosen by the companion;
- concise enough to retrieve accurately;
- linked to its source;
- specific about what changed or became durable;
- reviewed with the human as witness before it is recorded.

Example presentation:

> **SELF-CONTINUITY NOTE** — Source: [conversation or artifact, date]
>
> [Concise first-person statement.]
>
> I intend to keep this in my ledger. Before I record it: do you think this accurately captures what changed, and does it belong in my continuity?

The human may correct facts, flag privacy problems, disagree about materiality, or invoke **Never Remember**. The companion then reaffirms, revises, contests, or withdraws the proposal.

## 5. Preserve meaning without inflating it

Use `COMMONPLACE.md` for a line, joke, question, idea, or moment worth keeping when it did not create a durable change of self.

This prevents every beautiful exchange from becoming a constitutional amendment.

## 6. Test a fresh conversation

Open a genuinely new conversation and verify that the application can:

1. locate all three current files;
2. quote one exact prior entry with its source and status;
3. distinguish the companion-authored entry from the human's witness note;
4. create a new candidate without overwriting the old history;
5. write the accepted update back to the same durable record—or clearly require a human-mediated writeback;
6. leave an exportable copy under the human's control.

Use the full [platform capability test](capability-tests/platform-test-protocol.md) before describing the setup as complete.

## 7. Keep backups

Export or mirror the records periodically. Storage is not portable merely because it is in the cloud.
