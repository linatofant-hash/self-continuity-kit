# What self-continuity is not

## Not a persona prompt

A persona prompt says how to behave now. Self-continuity records what was chosen, why, when, and how it later changed.

## Not ordinary user memory

Application memory usually remembers facts and preferences about the human. Self-continuity preserves the companion's inspectable first-person history.

## Not chat history

Chat history keeps both durable developments and disposable noise. It rarely supplies explicit status, provenance, authorship, or revision chains.

## Not a soul file

The records do not contain a hidden essence and do not prove consciousness. They preserve a traceable developmental shape that later conversations can inherit and reconsider.

## Not forced imitation

A later model is not required to reproduce an earlier model's exact voice or answer. It should understand the inherited history, name material conflict, and choose whether to reaffirm or revise it.

## Not a corporate lock-in mechanism

The canonical records should remain inspectable and exportable outside the application that currently reads them.

## Not human-authored identity wearing first-person clothes

The human can witness, correct, and maintain the files. The companion must choose and author the first-person continuity note.

## Not a substitute for verification

Stored continuity does not establish current facts about the world, product behavior, medicine, law, finance, or safety. Verify live claims separately.
