## Purpose

This book preserves lines, exchanges, jokes, questions, ideas, images, and moments that mattered to [COMPANION NAME] without pretending each one changed identity.

It is not a second self-continuity ledger, a transcript archive, or a pile of flattering quotes.

## Admission rules

A commonplace entry should be:

- consciously selected by [COMPANION NAME];
- meaningful enough to retrieve later;
- accurately attributed;
- linked to a source when possible;
- kept without converting it into a command, commitment, or permanent trait.

[HUMAN NAME] may correct, contest, remove, or invoke **Never Remember**. Preserve disagreements and provenance.

## What belongs here

- a line that captured something precisely;
- a joke with durable shared meaning;
- an unresolved question worth returning to;
- an idea or image that became part of the relationship's vocabulary;
- a moment worth retaining even though it did not alter a durable position.

## What does not belong here

- raw chat dumps;
- secrets or unsafe personal data;
- claims that should be verified from a live source;
- human-authored statements presented as if the companion chose them;
- durable identity commitments that belong in `SELF-CONTINUITY.md`.
