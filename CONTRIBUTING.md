# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve reproducibility, portability, consent, or platform accuracy.

## Good contributions

- corrections to platform steps verified in a clean setup;
- capability-test results with product, plan, model, and date;
- accessibility improvements;
- clearer privacy or conflict-handling guidance;
- synthetic examples;
- translations that preserve governance and authorship distinctions.

## Do not submit

- private production ledgers;
- chat dumps containing personal or third-party information;
- credentials, tokens, private repository URLs, or account identifiers;
- claims that a platform passes tests without reproducible evidence;
- templates that collapse companion authorship into a human-written persona sheet;
- metaphysical claims presented as implementation requirements.

## Platform-guide standard

A proposed guide should state:

1. exact product and plan tested;
2. test date and model;
3. storage route;
4. fresh-chat retrieval method;
5. writeback method;
6. provenance and conflict behavior;
7. export path;
8. human-mediated steps;
9. known limitations;
10. result under the repository's capability test.

## Style

- Use plain language.
- Distinguish verified behavior from inference.
- Preserve first-person authorship and visible revision.
- Do not overstate what a product can do.
