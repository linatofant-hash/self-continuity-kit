# Claude implementation guide

**Status:** In development

We have a working external-vault implementation, but the public guide will be published only after it has been reduced to reproducible steps and rerun against the repository's capability test.

The final guide must document:

- canonical storage location;
- fresh-conversation discovery;
- complete reads rather than snippets;
- companion-authored entries;
- witness review and Never Remember;
- safe writeback with conflict handling;
- provenance and visible revision;
- export and migration;
- the difference between Claude chat, Projects, connectors, and external tool-backed storage.

Until then, use the platform-neutral templates and treat any manual copy/paste workflow as assisted rather than full writeback.
